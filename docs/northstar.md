# Brett Snyder Portfolio: North Star

## Mission

Present Brett Snyder as a Design Engineer and Product-Focused Frontend Developer through clear evidence of SaaS, React and Next.js, Shopify and CMS, conversion-focused web, design-system, and AI-assisted development work.

The portfolio should remain fast, personal, visually distinctive, and easy to maintain.

## Audience

Hiring managers and recruiters are the primary audience. Potential clients are secondary. Visitors should quickly understand Brett's role, see relevant work, reach the resume, and make contact.

## Success Criteria

- Brett's positioning and strongest work are clear within 30 seconds.
- A visitor can reach a relevant case study, resume, or contact path within one minute.
- Important content is available in the initial HTML response for people and crawlers.
- Public claims match the resume and visible project evidence.
- Content remains easy to update through the existing Markdown workflow.

## Non-Negotiables

1. Preserve the existing visual design, layouts, navigation, themes, Blog, Projects, and content engines.
2. Prefer targeted improvements over rebuilding working systems.
3. Keep content in Markdown with generated static data. Do not add a CMS or database.
4. Keep resume and cover-letter PDFs synchronized with their editable source files.
5. Maintain performance, responsive behavior, semantic markup, keyboard access, and reduced-motion support.
6. Do not introduce unsupported claims, hidden search content, unnecessary dependencies, or new public-facing em dashes.

## Voice

Professional, direct, personal, and specific. Explain real decisions and outcomes without corporate filler or exaggerated claims.
