Online Nursing Programs
 
 
In keeping with our 117-year history of academic excellence, Bradley University proudly announces the launch of five new online nursing programs.
  • Master of Science in Nursing – Nursing Administration (RN to MSN)
  • Master of Science in Nursing – Family Nurse Practitioner (BSN to MSN)
  • Family Nurse Practitioner Certificate (MSN to FNP)
  • Doctor of Nursing Practice – Family Nurse Practitioner (BSN to DNP)
  • Doctor of Nursing Practice – Leadership (MSN to DNP)
    By delivering the complete Bradley Experience in a cutting-edge online format, these innovative programs provide the flexibility and convenience you need to earn an advanced nursing degree while you balance the demands of work and family.
We help prepare you for leadership at every level.
  • Complete coursework is 100% online with no campus residency requirements.
  • Continue to work while you earn your degree.
  • Choose your site supervisor.
  • Complete clinicals locally.
  • Prepare yourself for the ANCC certification exam.
    Now, no matter where you live, you can come back to Bradley and define a clear path for your future.
